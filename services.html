Music Services

Coaching & Tutition

I offer one-to-one vocal coaching for singers at all levels — from emerging artists to seasoned professionals. My specialism lies in contemporary classical and experimental music, but I also love working on standard repertoire, audition material, and new roles.

Looking to develop a deeper understanding of music? I also offer tuititon in music theory and keyboard skills for any musicians looking to develop their musicianship chops.

FAQs

What services do you offer?

  • Coaching for professional singers: role prep, audition prep, accompanying recordings, English, German and Italian diction.
  • Expertise on new music: But where's the key signature?! Instead of struggling through a tricky contemporary score alone, Discover it with Dave.
  • Support for beginners: You don't feel confident in your weekly choir rehearsal? You've always dreamt of being able to stand up to sing to friends and family? Explore using your voice confidently in a friendly and supportive environment.
  • Musicianship and theory: Did these vital skills for musicians never really click? I can help you develop the mental and practical skills you need to understand and analyze the music you're working on.

Where are you located?

I'm based in Berlin and coach in various locations in the city. I can also offer online sessions depending on the nature of the work you want to do.

Will you play for my [audition/concert/wedding/...]?

Having a pianist you can trust is always worthwhile. I'm happy to play for your events depending on availability. Travel outside of Berlin is also possible.

Will you record a practice track?

Yes of course! If you need a pianist but don't like being in the same room as them I can also send you recordings of the music you're working on.

How much does all this cost?

My going rate is €50 per hour, although this can be more for demanding work (for example concerts or recordings). I can also offer a discount if you want to work more regularly. Feel free to get in touch if you have more specific questions about pricing.

Sheet Music Services

I create professional arrangements and transcriptions for singers, instrumentalists, ensembles, and composers. My experience as a conductor and composer means I understand what musicians need from a score: clarity, accuracy, and playability.

Pricing varies depending on the extent and complexity of the work. Please get in touch for more information.
