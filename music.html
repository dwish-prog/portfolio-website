About Me

I’m a Scottish pianist, conductor, and vocal coach specializing in contemporary classical opera and experimental music. Since 2019, I have worked as a Musical Director, Musical Assistant, and Souffleur (Prompter) on several projects as a guest at Deutsche Oper Berlin. From 2020-2025, I was a member of the ensemble at Theater Vorpommern in Stralsund, where I performed in, acted as musical director for, and accompanied dozens of shows. Over the past decade, I collaborated with a wide range of singers, composers, and ensembles throughout Europe.

Singers often remark on my calm, encouraging approach and my ability to make even the most rhythmically or harmonically complex scores feel approachable and expressive. My coaching focuses on clarity, musicality, and communication to help each performer find freedom and authenticity in performance.

Artistic Approach

I believe that music-making works best in an environment of curiosity and respect. I care deeply about treating my colleagues kindly and working in a way that encourages exploration and gives performers the freedom and confidence to take risks without being afraid of mistakes. My aim is to help musicians I collaborate with to try new things, and in the process, to fail, laugh, and create.

I think critically about what I do, questioning habits and traditions rather than following them blindly. While I have a strong grounding in the stylistic conventions of western classical music, I see them as tools, not constraints. I enjoy finding creative, rational, and sometimes unconventional solutions to musical challenges.

Above all, I try to keep music human. Humor, flexibility, and genuine collaboration matter to me as much as accuracy and discipline. Whether coaching a singer through a complex score, leading a rehearsal or accompanying on the piano, I want the process of music-making to feel alive, thoughtful, and shared — never robotic.
